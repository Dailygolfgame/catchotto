Privacy

Privacy policy.

Last updated: 4 November 2026

Catch Otto collects as little as possible. There are no accounts, no ads and no cookies.

  • Your progress (moves, stats, streaks and your character’s look) is saved in your browser’s local storage. It never leaves your device. Clear your site data to remove it.
  • Hosting. The site is hosted on GitHub Pages, which processes technical data such as your IP address to deliver the pages.
  • Visit statistics. GoatCounter counts page visits without cookies and without collecting personal data.
  • Contact form. If you write to us, Formspree delivers your name, email and message to us by email. We use them only to reply.

To see, correct or delete anything you have sent us, use the contact page. If this policy changes, the date above changes with it.
