    Introduction
    
A small daily puzzle about packing.

    
Tight Fit gives you a dark frame and a handful of pieces that are all the same shape. Turn them, flip them and fit every one of them into the frame. It sounds easy until the last piece does not fit.

  

  

    How to play
    
    
      
  1. Drag a piece to move it. Pieces snap to the grid.
    2. 
      
  2. Tap a piece to rotate it, and use Flip to mirror it.
    3. 
      
  3. Fill the frame. The puzzle is solved when every piece is inside.

    Questions
    
Is there always a solution? Yes, exactly one. Every puzzle is checked by a solver before you see it.

    
When is the next puzzle? At midnight in your time zone. Monday is easiest and the weekend is hardest.

    
Can I play more? Yes. Unlimited makes a new puzzle whenever you want, at the level you choose.
