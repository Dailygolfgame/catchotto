Tight Fit.

Every piece is the same shape. Fit them all into the dark frame. There is only one way.

How to play

  • Drag a piece to move it. Pieces snap to the grid.
  • Tap a piece to rotate it. Flip mirrors the selected piece.
  • On a keyboard: R rotates, F flips, and the arrow keys move the selected piece.
  • Each level has exactly one solution. Your progress is saved in your browser.
